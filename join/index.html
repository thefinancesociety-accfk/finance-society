Join

Earn your seat

Membership is open to students of every department of Amity University Kolkata.

Eligibility

Who can join

  • Students currently enrolled at Amity University Kolkata, in any department.
  • Any year and any programme. No prior finance experience is needed; interest and commitment are.
Expectations

  1. Show up

    Regular attendance at society sessions and workshops.

  2. Contribute

    Work on something during the semester: a note, a model, a workshop or an event.

  3. Hold the standard

    Professional conduct, and accurate sourcing in anything published in the society's name.

How to apply

One email

The button opens a draft to the society with the details we need already laid out. Fill in your name, enrolment number, course, semester and department, and send.

Apply by email →

On a computer the button opens Gmail in your browser; on a phone it opens your mail app. Prefer another mail app? Open it in your default mail app, or copy the template and email it to thefinancesocietyaccfk@gmail.com.
