About

A society built on work, not titles

The Finance Society is a student-run, faculty-mentored society based at Amity College of Commerce and Finance, Kolkata, and open to students across Amity University Kolkata.

Mission

Closing the gap between theory and practice

Our curriculum gives students strong theoretical grounding. The society exists to add what recruiters look for alongside it: the ability to build a model, read an annual report, form a view on a market and present it clearly.

Members leave each year with work they can point to, not just events they attended.

The programme

What members do in a semester

  • Skill workshops

    A training ladder from Excel for finance to three-statement modelling, DCF valuation, reading annual reports and ratio analysis.

  • Research

    Company analyses and sector notes written by members, reviewed for sourcing and format, and published on this site.

  • Markets

    Discussion of markets, policy and current affairs, with quizzes and debates that reward preparation and clear argument.

  • Guest sessions

    Talks and interactions with practitioners and alumni, arranged through the Faculty Mentor.

Charter summary · proposed structure, subject to approval

How the society runs

Authority flows down from the faculty layer; accountability flows up through the Council. Every post carries defined deliverables, and advancement is earned on demonstrated work, not seniority of batch.

  • Faculty Advisory Committee

    Academic oversight of all activity. Approves the annual plan, the event calendar and expenditure, and chairs the selection of office bearers.

  • Core Executive Council

    Seven posts with single charters: President, Vice-President, Treasurer, and Heads of Modelling & Training, Research & Analysis, Events & Outreach, and Marketing & Media. Office is held for one academic year.

  • Associates

    Six posts carrying the standing work of each function: training, research, editorial review, events, design and records.

  • Directorate

    A merit tier, conferred each semester on members who have delivered. Each Director owns one named initiative and mentors newer members. It carries no line authority.

  • Financial controls

    Every collection is receipted, payments need two signatures, and the books are placed before the Faculty Mentor each semester.
