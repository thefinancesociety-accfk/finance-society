Finance Society

A student society that turns classroom finance into rigorous research, an informed view of markets, and careers built on demonstrable work.

Join the Society Our work
What we do

Three pillars

I

Research

Company analyses, sector notes and financial models written by members, reviewed for sourcing and published under the society's name.

II

Markets

Regular discussion of markets, policy and the headlines that move them, alongside quizzes, debates and guest sessions.

III

Careers

Practical skills for finance roles, from Excel and three-statement modelling to valuation, built into a portfolio of work members can show.
