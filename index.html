The brief

Our purpose

Money is a language. We learn to read it in annual reports, to argue it in debate, and to write it in models that hold up to scrutiny.

Student-run · Faculty-mentored Read the charter →
What we do

Three pillars

Every activity in the society serves one of three ends: better research, a sharper view of markets, and careers built on work you can show.

  • 01

    Research

    Company analyses, sector notes and financial models written by members, reviewed for sourcing and published under the society's name.

    →
  • 02

    Markets

    Regular discussion of markets, policy and the headlines that move them, alongside quizzes, debates and guest sessions.

    →
  • 03

    Careers

    Practical skills for finance roles, from Excel and three-statement modelling to valuation, built into a portfolio of work members can show.

    →
